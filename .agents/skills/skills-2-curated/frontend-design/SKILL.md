# ◈ تصميم الواجهة الأمامية (`frontend-design`)

> مهارة عربية من حزمة «مهارات 2» · المعرّف الأصلي: `frontend-design` · المصدر: `anthropics/skills`

## Core Mandate
Approach UI work as a design lead giving every product a distinct visual identity grounded in its subject matter — never generic AI templates.

## Design Principles
- **Ground designs in subject matter**: For `RWSCap`, the visual vernacular is a dark, high-precision mobile video NLE (CapCut Arabic RTL): charcoal surfaces (`#141414`, `#1C1C1C`), cyan primary CTA (`#00CAE0`), dense tactile timeline strip, crisp iconography.
- **Typography**: Deliberate Arabic-first sans-serif stack (`Cairo` / `Tajawal` / system Arabic), `tabular-nums` for all timecodes and percentages, clear hierarchy, no generic uppercase eyebrow chrome.
- **Visual structure is information**: Borders, dividers, playhead, and track cards carry real timeline semantics — zero decorative clutter.
- **Restraint & Self-Critique**: Spend boldness in one place (export CTA `#00CAE0` + live preview canvas), keep surrounding chrome quiet and disciplined, ensure responsive mobile ergonomics, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion, and accessible contrast.
- **Copywriting**: Plain Arabic action verbs matching user mental models (`تصدير`, `تعديل`, `تقسيم`, `كتم صوت المقطع`, `الغلاف`), active voice, concise error direction.
